Assistant Prototype

Material, without the background light: white surfaces with an inner highlight and a soft two-step shadow on a flat ground, one big number leading each screen. The Ring accent and Brightline palette stay. A gradient edge marks the assistant. It drifts slowly along the border all the time; a soft glow behind it brightens while the assistant works and blooms once when an answer lands. The money stays plain: the figures, the table, the confirm sheet and the Retry button carry no AI styling. It waits for you: click the reply field under the figures, press its send button, then pick each follow-up. The chapters jump to any step.

What happens
  • Home. The assistant's weekly briefing: a headline, one large figure with its trend, the ask field, then three insight cards. Each card is one number and one sentence. Payouts leads at twice the size, with the customers affected and the page's only button; the other two stay quiet. Clicking a card asks the assistant about it. Click the ask field and you type in place; press send and it drops to the bottom as the assist bar while the thinking starts.
  • Query. The glow brightens and the ring mark turns. The question rises into the title, the work runs in the open one line per step, then folds into one line that says what was read, with a “4 steps” toggle to see the query.
  • Build. The answer composes: the sentence, the figure counting down from last week, the gap splitting into causes, the chart drawing itself with a lit head that pops each week's point as it passes, then the recoverable band rising with its amount counting up. The prepared retry arrives with the same drifting edge and a glow that blooms once. The composer glow blooms and settles.
  • Drill down. When an answer is done, the assistant proposes the next question inside the bar, in its own violet, with a Tab key. Click it or press Tab and it rises into a new section. The 19 payments slide in and tick themselves selected while the total climbs to $4,212.00.
  • Retry. “Retry them” is proposed the same way. The retry is prepared, waiting for your approval. Review and retry opens the confirm sheet, which has no AI styling: the commit belongs to the platform. Approve to see the receipt, with a tonal button to cancel the rest for 10 minutes.

The three decisions I'm least sure of
  • The gradient ends in Heritage orange, the same orange as the Retry button. It ties the brand together, and it may also suggest the assistant owns the action. Ending the gradient at Burst Light would separate them.
  • Movement at rest. The edge never stops drifting, slowly, so the assistant always looks present. Copilot's shipped composer is neutral grey until something happens; if the drift starts to feel like wallpaper, it could hold still at rest and drift only while working.
  • The count-down on the figure. It explains the drop, but motion on a money figure can read as theatrical; a quieter version would just fade it in.

Direction 1 of six. The drifting edge and glow follow IBM Carbon's light metaphor and Microsoft Copilot's loading gradient, slowed down; the bloom on arrival takes the place of Copilot's one-shot Flair stroke. Brightline palette: ink #151738, Burst blue #3144B7, Burst Light #9EAAFC, Heritage #FE6200, tone #F2F2F9; Figtree stands in for Proxima Nova. Halden Supply Co., its customers and Westmoor Bank are fictional; the figures add up.
