Layout Comparison

Brief. Halden's finance lead asks why payouts dropped. The one job of the screen is to explain the drop and act on the part that can be recovered. First thing seen: the payout figure and the gap. Register: bright, spacious, direct. References: Ramp and Stripe's dashboard, airy density.

Visual rules, same in all three. Host Grotesk at one weight; hierarchy comes from size alone (64 / 52 / 36 / 30 / 18 / 15 / 13). White surfaces on cool grey, separated by tone, with no shadows. One brand colour, cobalt, on the primary button, links and the selected state. Recoverable money is a green word. Only the layout changes between the three.

The three decisions I'm least sure of

  1. Cobalt as the brand colour. It is a placeholder for a brand that isn't defined yet; Ramp and Stripe both get their confidence from one saturated colour, and this needs one too, but not necessarily this one.
  2. In B, where the prepared action lives. Top right of the canvas makes it the second thing you see, next to the figure. That is strong for a finance lead and may be too pushy for an answer they only asked to understand.
  3. In C, the three figure cards. They repeat what the sentence says. They earn their place only if people skim the cards and skip the sentence.
