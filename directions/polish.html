The three decisions I'm least sure of

  1. In A, dropping cards entirely. It is the strongest break from the default look, and it asks a lot of the rules and the grid; on a busy week it could feel sparse rather than composed.
  2. In B, going dark. Brightline is a light product. Instrument would need a light twin, and the dark version is what makes it feel precise.
  3. In C, the light at the top. It is the most ownable use of the gradient so far, and the closest to the decorative AI glow the research warned about. It has to stay behind the content, never on the money.
