Assistant Directions

What stays fixed. The original form: one centred conversation, composer at the bottom, UI composed inside the answer. The Brightline palette: white and #F2F2F9 grounds, ink #151738, Burst blue #3144B7 for links, Heritage orange #FE6200 for the one action. Figtree in place of Proxima Nova, 400 and 600.

What varies. How the assistant's own elements look: the composer, the working state, the label on what it made, and the action it prepared. The gradient runs Burst blue → Burst Light → Heritage, so the AI colour is the brand's, not the industry's purple.

One rule in all six. AI styling marks what the assistant is doing or has prepared, and never touches the money. The figures, the table and the Retry button stay plain, because a commit has to look like the platform, not like the model.

The three decisions I'm least sure of

  1. Leaving the indigo-purple out. Every option builds its gradient from Burst blue to Heritage orange so it reads as this brand. If the network wants the AI to have its own colour apart from the brand, that changes all six.
  2. Keeping the gradient off the Retry button. It is the most tempting place for it and the one I'd argue hardest against: the commit is the platform's, and in finance a glowing money button implies the model is moving the money.
  3. How much motion is left once the answer settles. The research points one way: in 2026 Microsoft 365 Copilot went largely black and white and SAP's Joule Work asks for restraint, so colour is kept for short state moments. Every option here goes still when the work is done; the frames show the working moment, so they look busier than the product would most of the time.
